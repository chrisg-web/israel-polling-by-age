Young Britons are five times less likely than over-65s to side with Israel

In YouGov's June 2026 tracker, 5% of 18–24s say their sympathies lie more with the Israelis, against 25% of over-65s. On the right, Reform and Conservative voters are the only groups that lean towards Israel. No published UK poll splits right-wing voters by age.
