Young Republicans are far cooler on Israel than older Republicans

Six polls from late 2023 to 2026 ask about Israel in different ways. In every one, younger Republicans are less pro-Israel than older Republicans, by 14 to 40 points. On Pew's latest measure, only 28% of Republicans aged 18–34 view the Israeli government favourably, against 68% of those aged 50 and over.
