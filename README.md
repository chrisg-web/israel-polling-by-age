# Israel polling by age

Two chart pages comparing younger and older people's views of Israel:

- **United States** (`index.html`): younger vs older Republicans, from Pew, the University of Maryland, JCFA and Northeastern University (2023–2026).
- **Great Britain** (`uk.html`): YouGov's sympathies tracker by age (2019–2026), with Conservative and Reform voters from YouGov and More in Common.

Sources are listed at the bottom of each page.
